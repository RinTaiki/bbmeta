About BBmeta

BBmeta is a community-built stats platform for Badland Brawl, powered by the official Public API.

What we offer

  • Player profiles with rank, trophies, win rate, and battle history
  • Global leaderboard
  • Meta decks with trophy-bucket filtering
  • Clone stats, including usage and win rates
  • Season recaps

This is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Frogmind.

Built by a solo developer who loves the game and wanted better tools for the community.
